## Main conclusion

The site is a useful Grade 9 foundation, but it cannot yet be called a complete preparation pack for October mocks. Public IB material specifies a flexible framework and assessment skills, not one universal Grade 9 sequence or the contents of a particular school's October paper. The exact October date and mainly-Grade-9 emphasis come from Dmitry; no current teacher-issued October scope was found in the local sources inspected.

School-generated tasks and external eAssessment are distinct; see [IB assessment](https://ibo.org/programmes/middle-years-programme/assessment-and-exams/). NLCS confirms separate sciences, Mathematics, language subjects, humanities, Arts/Design, Personal Project and an interdisciplinary examination in the Upper School, but its public page does not give Luka's October paper specifications. See [NLCS Upper School](https://nlcsdubai.ae/ib-upper-school-grades-school-dubai/).

## Source qualifications that matter

1. **Biology conflict:** the local Grade 9 Topic List explicitly says Unit 5 Variation and Evolution was not assessed in that EOY. The old handoff describes later teacher-driven additions. The map retains the conflict instead of pretending either historical statement proves October scope. Genetics from the Grade 8 recap is a separate item.
2. **Mathematics:** [IB distinguishes Standard and Extended](https://ibo.org/programmes/middle-years-programme/curriculum/mathematics/). Existing resources mix levels; a local Standard practice pack does not prove the student's current exam registration. Confirm level and teacher inclusion before excluding or prioritizing advanced topics.
3. **French:** Capable is the level of one recovered Grade 9 source, not verified current registration. [The 2020 IB language acquisition brief](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/brochures-and-infographics/pdfs/myp-brief-language-acquisition-2020-en.pdf) covers listening, reading, speaking and writing. A transcript exercise is not listening practice.
4. **English:** the recovered school booklet states the importance of unseen texts and includes literary/non-literary writing. Macbeth remains useful school practice, not a promised future exam text.
5. **IDL:** the [current linked interdisciplinary brief](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/brochures-and-infographics/pdfs/myp-brief-interdisciplinary-learning_en.pdf) uses Evaluating, Synthesizing and Reflecting. No session theme or subject pairing is guessed. Some public briefs contain old blueprint wording; current school/session instructions take precedence over old task counts.
6. The [science brief](https://ibo.org/globalassets/new-structure/brochures-and-infographics/pdfs/myp-brief-sciences-en.pdf) supports knowledge, investigation, data analysis and application as preparation areas. A topic can be in the framework without appearing in a particular paper.
