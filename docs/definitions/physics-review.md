# Physics definition review

Reviewed 6 October 2026. English and Russian explanations are independently authored paraphrases, not quotations or claimed publisher translations. Source labels identify the language of the source page.

The review covers the legacy physics glossary, concept cards and extensions, the nuclear-physics glossary and lessons, and repeated practice explanations. It distinguishes displacement from path length, velocity from speed, gravitational weight from Russian school «вес», heat from temperature, energy from power, proportionality from elasticity, and activity from detector counts. Nuclear equations balance nucleon number and charge in the studied processes; they also require conservation of energy and momentum. Equal nucleon counts do not imply equal total rest masses.

Four original diagrams show a scaled path/displacement triangle, equal-temperature samples with different energy content, ammeter/voltmeter placement, and transverse-wave amplitude/wavelength. Each has a bilingual title, description and caption. Existing interactive diagrams are linked to their school-model conditions.
