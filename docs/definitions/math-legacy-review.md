# Legacy mathematics definition review

The existing English and Russian teaching text was reviewed in place. Definitions, examples, practice answers and interactive explanations were checked together; the review does not treat a separate glossary as a substitute for correcting the lesson.

The review covers 51 definition cards, four prose extensions, 13 interactive explanations, 68 practice problems and eight mock lessons. Source-language labels identify the original evidence language. Every displayed explanation is an authored paraphrase; Russian adaptations of English sources are not Russian quotations.

## Scope and conventions

Natural numbers start at 1 in the number-set activity. Functions distinguish domain, range and codomain. Real principal roots and restricted exponent laws state their domains. Quartiles use medians of the two halves, excluding an odd central median; grouped percentiles use linear interpolation and are estimates. Standard deviation distinguishes a full population from a sample. Geometric pictures state when they use a right triangle, a minor arc, a flat map or a restricted model.

Sources verify concepts and methods; worked examples and diagrams are authored for this site. Numerical checks cover rational-equation roots, quartiles and cumulative-frequency interpolation, exact number classification, normalised scientific notation and the intersecting-chord construction.
