## Language banks and remaining evidence limits

The lexical bank inventory is separate from concept-definition blocks: [English rows](english-lexical-rows.json), [French rows, idioms and conjugations](french-lexical-rows.json), [Arabic rows and alphabet](arabic-lexical-rows.json). It itemizes 269 row occurrences; a row may contain a pair of words or a phrase. These are not 269 unique terms across all repeated banks.

French contains 73 reference rows and 12 phrase rows, not the earlier reported 75. Arabic contains 51 reference rows and 12 phrases, not 53, plus 45 canonical lesson-word rows. The old figures were counting mistakes; no vocabulary rows were deleted. Twelve French paradigms contain 72 forms. The Arabic table contains 28 letters with contextual forms. These tables are existing structural representations, not newly drawn figures.

Every lexical row was reviewed for meaning, inflection and register and now has read source evidence for its selected contextual meaning: 76 English, 85 French and 108 Arabic rows. The 217 formerly editorial-only rows were closed separately: 60 English dictionary-sense checks; 70 French checks (69 direct contextual matches and one polite request supported compositionally); and 87 Arabic checks (49 direct lexical/phrase matches and 38 combined lexical plus morphology/context matches). The final row inventories identify the evidence method and retained scope limits. A general grammar citation alone is never credited as a word-meaning receipt, and a derived form or composed phrase is not presented as a verbatim dictionary quotation.

Russian equivalents remain authored translations. The French closure additionally records publisher multilingual corroboration for 30 complete and 15 partial Russian equivalents; those are not independent Russian-language dictionary definitions. The broad literary themes/dates marked in the French supplementary inventory are cultural context, not unverified scientific definitions. Browser-generated Arabic pronunciation is not certified by this text audit.

Historical geography details retained from the case-study pages are outside this terminology count and are not treated as term errors. Authored mathematical derivations (rounding bounds, grouped interpolation and sector-minus-triangle area) are identified as derivations, not quotations. An indexed-only Russian exponential-function source is not labelled a directly retrieved page.

Deployment verification is recorded with the release commit and CI run.

No 100% correctness claim is inferred from 153 routes or successful tests. The finite semantic review, external source evidence, editorial lexical checking and browser verification are distinct forms of evidence.
