## Source clarification follow-up — 2026-10-15

Local candidate correction, pending independent review and final render; not a release receipt. Finite-interval activity is explicitly average activity; the detector example accounts for background and detection fraction. Half-life applies to the parent radionuclide activity, with possible daughter contribution. The background definition includes natural and human-made sources. Russian fission working bridges N to English P without changing the numerical example. IDs, matchers and practice values are retained.
