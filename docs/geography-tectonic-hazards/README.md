## Evidence and interpretation

The supplied checklist names countries and volcano but no case years. The pack transparently uses Chile 2010, Nepal 2015 and Eyjafjallajökull 2010. Chile and Nepal match the prior local bilingual geography essay plan; that is continuity evidence, not separate teacher confirmation. Historical offline Geography inventories and revision documents were checked, but no complete offline tectonics course was located. Scientific processes and event facts were checked against public primary sources and research, linked per lesson and in the reference page.

Casualty estimates retain rounded or source-specific definitions. Chile's 525 is a combined earthquake-tsunami disaster total; Nepal's roughly 9,000 is a rounded sequence/disaster total. Monetary losses, recovery needs and affected homes are not treated as interchangeable. Aviation passenger journeys are not described as unique individuals.

Content distinguishes mostly solid mantle from liquid outer core, water-assisted mantle-wedge melting from friction melting, continental underthrusting from volcanic-arc subduction, magnitude from intensity, crater from caldera, and early warning from prediction. Building design addresses residual risk and functional recovery as well as life safety. Practice does not produce an IB grade or pretend to reproduce a teacher markscheme.

The source DOCX, local evidence and private provenance stay outside this public repository. All explanatory text, practice and SVG diagrams were authored for the pack.
