# Definition and explanation revision — 6 October 2026

The revision covers terminology and repeated explanations throughout all eight subjects, the three dedicated preparation packs, the 18 shared practice lessons and shared pages. The finite route inventory contains 153 routes. This is a content scope, not a claim about a future examination paper.

## Before and after

The baseline inventory identified 525 named definition/rule blocks in each language. Of the 1,050 EN/RU occurrences, 1,038 have changed primary wording. The 12 retained occurrences are four IB criterion blocks per language, an electromagnetic-spectrum simulator label per language and an enrichment film/music list per language. ## Source and language practice

Sources name their language and relevant section. English and Russian explanations are authored paraphrases; a Russian interface does not imply that a cited English source is a published Russian definition. Russian educational sources were independently consulted where available, and Russian adaptations of English-only material are labelled accordingly. Source reading receipts and exact private before/after snapshots are kept outside this public repository.

Distinctions include expression/equation, term/factor/coefficient, sector/segment, objective/optimal solution, speed/velocity, mass/weight, precision/accuracy, acid strength/concentration, respiration/fermentation and root/derived verb pattern. Model assumptions and exceptions accompany the simplified classroom descriptions.

Original diagrams distinguish related ideas or show a physical, geometric or grammatical relationship. Dense diagrams provide keyboard-accessible horizontal scrolling to preserve readable labels on small screens. Diagrams and examples are not reproduced textbook pages.

## Review and verification limits

Each subject had a separate owner and independent peer review. Peer review records specify their finite semantic, numerical and source-check coverage; a schema check or a working page is not treated as proof that its content is correct. English/French/Arabic peer review, for example, deeply checked 49 high-risk concepts rather than claiming a second reading of every text leaf.

Elementary French/Arabic glosses were editorially checked for contextual meaning and morphology; they are not represented as individually retrieved dictionary quotations. Geography key case figures and identified legacy errors were rechecked. Some inherited historical details, including the IATA loss estimate and precise Iceland eruption endpoints, were not independently reverified in this definition revision. See the subject notes for scope.
